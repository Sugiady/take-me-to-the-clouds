# Flight Log ✈️

This log grows together with the ground-school project.

## Virtual Flight 001 — KAVX · Catalina Airport / Airport in the Sky

**Unlocked by:** first clean 10/10 quiz  
**Theme:** first virtual fly-out date  
**Destination:** Santa Catalina Island, California

We imagined flying into Catalina's hilltop general-aviation airport, then heading down toward Avalon and the coast for a slow afternoon off from ground school.

### Why it belongs here
The point of the log is not to pretend a virtual trip is a real flight. It is a record of the places we use as rewards, scenarios, and motivation while learning.

## Mission 001 — First Real Discovery Flight

**Status:** Locked · within reach  
**Preferred areas:** Seattle or Los Angeles  
**Crew concept:** human CFI / pilot flying; AI companion only as a quiet observer and post-flight debrief partner.

### What to record
- airport and aircraft
- date / duration
- first control inputs
- a few instrument readings the student chooses to note
- what the view felt like
- what turbulence / turns / climb / descent felt like in the body
- questions that appeared in flight
- post-flight lesson links

When completed, this mission becomes:

**REAL FLIGHT LOG 001**

The goal is not real-time AI flight advice. During a discovery flight, the instructor and normal cockpit priorities come first. The companion role is deliberately lightweight: enjoy the view, capture a few memories, and unpack them together after landing.
